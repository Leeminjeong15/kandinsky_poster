Komposition VIII

Point Line Plane

The Work

Kandinsky painted Komposition VIII in 1923, while he was teaching at the Bauhaus in Weimar. Circles, triangles and straight lines float on a light ground, like notes in a piece of music. He saw it as a high point of his work after the war.

The Artist

Wassily Kandinsky (1866–1944) was born in Moscow and is known as one of the first painters of abstract art. He believed colours and shapes could be heard like sounds, and explained this in his book Point and Line to Plane (1926).
